Drive to every parkrun

Drive times are free-flow car routes from OpenRouteService, from each athlete's neighbourhood centroid, with no traffic. ⛴ marks a parkrun that can only be reached by Channel Tunnel or ferry. The router times the crossing at sailing speed and does not say which one it chose. It leaves out check-in (30 minutes or more for the tunnel, 45 to 90 for a ferry), waiting for a departure, and overnight sailings, so those times are too short. Parkruns with no road route (outside Europe, or islands with no car ferry) are listed with blanks. Click a row to find it on the map, or a column heading to sort.
